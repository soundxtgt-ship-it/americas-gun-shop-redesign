Privacy

What this preview stores

Last updated August 21, 2026.

No form or payment collection

This redesigned catalog does not accept orders, payments, uploads, reservations, account registrations, or contact-form submissions. Contact and shopping links take you to the established official store, whose own terms and privacy practices apply.

Information saved on your device

The site uses browser local storage for two optional features: your light or dark theme choice and the sample products in your saved pickup list. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to the shop by this site. You can clear it through your browser settings or by clearing the pickup list.

External services

Font files are requested from Fontshare. The location page includes a Google Maps embed. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. External links to the official store, Google Maps, ATF, and Pennsylvania government websites are governed by those sites' policies.

Hosting logs

The final hosting provider may keep routine security and access logs. Confirm the provider's retention and privacy settings before launch and update this notice if analytics, advertising, forms, accounts, or other data collection are added.

Questions

For privacy questions about America's Gun Shop, call (610) 556-0223 or use the official contact form (opens in a new tab).
