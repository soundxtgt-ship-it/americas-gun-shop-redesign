Service 01

FFL Transfers

Bought a firearm online or from an out-of-state seller? We'll receive it as your licensed FFL dealer and complete the required background check when you pick it up.

  1. Contact the shop before the seller ships the item.
  2. Confirm the current transfer fee and required seller paperwork.
  3. Wait for the shop to tell you the item is ready for pickup.
  4. Complete all required identification, paperwork, and background-check steps in store.

Do not arrange shipment until the shop confirms it can accept the transfer. Fees and requirements vary by item and transaction.
