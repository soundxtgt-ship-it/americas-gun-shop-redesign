Background checks

Every firearm sale requires a NICS or PA-PICS background check and completion of ATF Form 4473 in person at our store. Learn more from the Pennsylvania State Police firearms division.

Age & ID requirements

You must be 21+ to purchase a handgun or handgun ammunition, and 18+ for long guns, with valid PA-accepted government-issued ID. We reserve the right to refuse any sale.

Safe storage

Pennsylvania law requires safe transport of firearms and imposes liability for unsecured firearms accessible to minors. Ask our staff about lock boxes and storage options.
