Background checks

Every firearm sale requires a NICS or PA-PICS background check and completion of ATF Form 4473 in person at our store. Learn more from the Pennsylvania State Police firearms division.

Age & ID requirements

Federal minimum ages generally differ for handguns and long guns, and state or local rules may add restrictions. Valid government-issued identification is required; eligibility is confirmed in store.

Safe storage

Store and transport firearms responsibly and in accordance with applicable law. Ask our staff about lock boxes, safes, and storage options suited to your household.
